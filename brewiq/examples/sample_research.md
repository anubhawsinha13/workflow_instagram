# Research

## Topic
Check an AI summary against a public source

## Category
try_this

## Timeliness
evergreen

## Headline
Show the source, then the summary

## Hook
A short summary is useful only when you can see what it left out.

## Why it matters
Long pages are hard to skim. An AI summary can save time, and it can also drop the caveat that changes the meaning.

## The point
Ask for the summary, then ask what a reader still has to open the source to confirm. NIST keeps a public page titled AI Risk Management Framework.

## Event date
Not a single news event.

## Publication date
The page title was read from the public NIST page. This note does not add a release date that was not taken from that page.

## Claims
- NIST publishes a public page titled AI Risk Management Framework. | https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework | AI Risk Management Framework | page title read from the public page | not a single news event

## Availability
The NIST page is public. You do not need a paid account to open it.

## Limitations
This prompt does not measure how often a chatbot follows it. A prompt is not proof that the summary is right.

## Try it
Summarize this page in five sentences. Then list three things a reader still needs to open the source to confirm. If you cannot open the page, say so instead of guessing. Page: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

## Product
a general chat assistant
